## 1. The Formal Aesthetic Law: "Minecraft at First Glance, Vigorous Monster on Second Look"

AI-generated Blockbench models fail in **two opposite directions** when they lack a formal medium philosophy:

| Failure / Mastery Zone | Geometric Habit | Surface / Pixel Habit | Viewer Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Left Trap: Procedural Toy-Stick Minimalism** | 1 axis-aligned stick per limb; tall stilt legs; flat brick paws with 3 clone-peg toes; surface-glued "Lego-stud" stickers; generic box-snout templates. | Flat fills, grey brightness-only ramps, or rigid concentric "plastic" border outlines that reset on every cube face. | *"Looks like a stiff, cheap procedural toy or 2013 mod mob."* |
| **Center Target: Vigorous Carved Blockbench (`Anchors 01–09`)** | **Honest, proud cuboids** (`90°`, `45°`, `30°`, `22.5°`, `15°` planar box facets) **packed 40–85% deep into compound anatomical masses**; low-slung coiled center-of-gravity; rhythmic echeloned carapace/osteoderm rows; wide splayed multi-phalanx talons; 3D vaulted full-chord alpha wings. | Crisp `16 px/block` square texels (`Per-Face UV`), `45–50°` hue-shifted ramps, **cross-cube transverse scale-rungs, muscle crevices, corrugated ribs, and native mossy/stone pixel clusters** wrapping seamlessly across 3D seams. | *"Oh hey — this definitely belongs to Minecraft, yet it feels massive, muscular, dangerous, and masterfully sculpted!"* |
| **Right Trap: Fake-Blender Mesh / Curve Abuse (STRICTLY FORBIDDEN)** | Slicing shapes into dozens of `0.1–0.4u` micro-stepped staircases to fake smooth $C^2$ NURBS curves; `12–24`-band fake spheres/cylinders on organic bodies; irregular decimal cube sizes (`4.13 x 6.27`); vertex-pulled non-cuboid soup. | Blurry high-res airbrushing (`64x–512x`), stretched non-square texels, noisy per-pixel dither speckle, semi-transparent feathered alpha fringes. | *"Someone tried to use Blockbench as a crippled Blender substitute and destroyed the Minecraft soul."* |

### The Four Pillars of Vigorous Carved Blockbench
1. **Blockbench Is Blockbench — Honour the Cube:** Never hide the cuboid nature of the medium. Every individual element must remain a clean, readable `0.5u`/integer rectangular box or a `100%`-binary-alpha plane at `16 px/block`. Curvature is *implied* by the **interplay of bold angled facets (`10°–45°`) and optical pixel-relief carving**, never by slicing a curve into 20 tiny wafer steps (`G11`).
2. **Compound Cuboid Mass-Packing (Not Surface Stickers):** Master artists do not glue thin plates onto the outside of an empty rectangular tube. They sculpt anatomical bulk (bull necks, deltoids, trapezius humps, drumstick thighs, gular sacs, armored visors) by **sinking `3–7` medium-to-large cuboids `40–85%` deep into each other** so only bold stepped ridges, brows, and muscle bellies emerge from the shared silhouette.
3. **Coiled Predatory Weight & Grounded Stance (`G12`):** Heavy monsters, brutes, amphibians, and Elder Dragons carry weight *low and wide*. Grounded quadrupeds and wing-arm elders tuck their belly and chest low between wide-planted, thick-muscled limbs (`belly_clearance <= 0.35–0.45x shoulder_height`), gripping the earth with broad `4–5` digit splayed paws (`1.8–2.4x` wrist width).
4. **Cross-Cube Pixel-Grid Relief Carving (`16 px/block`):** Half of the "carved" sensation in master Blockbench art is **optical relief painted across clean cuboids** — rhythmic `2–3 px` transverse scale-rungs wrapping `3–5 px` down adjacent side faces, deep anatomical muscle clefts, glowing elemental fissures nested inside dark crevices, and `2–4 px` vanilla-kin material clusters (mossy stone, obsidian, bone, amethyst).

## 4. Formal 6-Stage Vision & 3D Spatial Reasoning Curriculum (For AI Agents & Small-Model Art Training)

To train smaller vision-language / spatial models (or guide an LLM artist step-by-step) to convert 2D creature references into **Vigorous Carved Blockbench** models *without* collapsing into toy sticks or fake-Blender curves, structure spatial reasoning as **six explicit intermediate representations (`S1 -> S6`)**:

### Stage `S1` — World-Scale Envelope, Center-of-Gravity Triangle & Stance Archetype
- **Visual Input:** Side, Front, and `3/4` reference views overlaid on a `16 px = 1 block` grid ruler.
- **Spatial Reasoning Target:**
  1. Classify Stance Archetype: `coiled_prowler` (`anchor_09`), `cantilevered_brute` (`anchor_07`), `sumo_sprawl` (`anchor_08`), `regal_quadruped` (`anchor_06`), `upright_elder` (`anchor_05`), or `serpentine_leviathan`.
  2. Measure `belly_clearance_ratio = Y_belly_min / Y_shoulder_top` (enforce `<= 0.42` for coiled/heavy beasts; `<= 0.18` for sumo amphibians) and `support_polygon_width / chest_width` (`>= 1.35` so wide-planted paws visibly grip the floor).
  3. Solve the **12-Point Proportional Ratio Matrix (`R1–R12`)** in Cranium-Units (`1 CU`).

### Stage `S2` — Honest Compound Cuboid Mass-Packing Graph (Anti-Blender Decomposition)
- **Cognitive Rule:** Never approximate an organic contour by slicing perpendicular cross-sections (`N > 5` thin rings). Instead, decompose each anatomical region (`skull`, `neck`, `thorax`, `limb_segment`) into **`1` Host Core Cuboid + `2–6` Sunken Muscular/Bevel Cuboids**:
  $$\text{MassCluster}(R) = \Big\{ B_{\text{core}}, \; \big(B_k, \; \mathbf{p}_k, \; (\theta_x, \theta_y, \theta_z)_k, \; \rho_{\text{bury}, k} \in [0.40, 0.82]\big)_{k=1}^{m} \Big\}$$
- Each secondary cuboid $B_k$ must have clean `0.5u`/integer dimensions, `min_edge >= 1.5u` on hero/boss bodies, and a named anatomical role (`deltoid_swell`, `trapezius_wedge`, `mandible_ramus`, `stepped_belly_tier_2`).

### Stage `S3` — Directional Echelon & Silhouette-Rhythm Vectors
- Parameterize repeating surface armor (`dorsal osteoderms`, `carapace roof-tiles`, `chin spikes`, `feather plane tiers`) as **coherent directional sequences** rather than uncoordinated boxes:
  - `flow_curve` (3D spline along brow -> spine -> tail, or wing arm),
  - `scale_envelope(t)` (e.g., `1.0 -> 1.85` peak at withers -> `0.45` tail taper),
  - `shared_lean_deg(t)` (`25° -> 48°` progressive backward rake),
  - `overlap_ratio` (`0.30–0.50` shingle overlap).

### Stage `S4` — Extremity Ground-Grip & True Hinge Topology
- Place every bone pivot **at the biological center of rotation before posing** (`hip -> forward femur -> backward tibia -> angled metatarsus -> heavy paw_base`).
- Branch hero/boss hands and feet into **`4–5` splayed multi-phalanx digits** (`fan_spread = 25°–45°`, `middle_digit = 1.25x outer_digit`, `proximal -> distal -> hooked_claw_block` + opposable thumb or rear dewclaw).

### Stage `S5` — The 3-Way Relief Split Boundary (`Cube` vs. `Alpha Plane` vs. `Transverse Pixel Carving`)
Teach the model an explicit geometric stopping criterion so it knows **when to stop adding cubes**:
1. **Does it change the thick 3D silhouette by `>= 1.5u` across `>= 2` views?** -> **Build as Sunken/Stepped Cuboid (`S2/S3`)**.
2. **Is its true physical thickness `< 1.0u` (`membranes`, `feathers`, `frills`, `fur tufts`, `tattered hems`)?** -> **Build as `100%` Hard-Alpha Plane (`layer`/`inflate` staggered)**.
3. **Is it rhythmic surface corrugation, muscle striation, scale banding, horn runes, or `<= 1.0u` dermal texture?** -> **Keep the Cuboid Clean & Carve in `16x` Pixel Paint** (transverse scale-rungs wrapping `3–5 px` across side edges, `1 px` rim over `1 px` cast shadow, `45–50°` hue-shifted AO).

### Stage `S6` — Multi-View Adversarial Render-to-Reference Loop
- Render `front`, `side`, `top`, `3/4 front`, `no-wing 4-view`, and `zoomed region crops` after each stage; compare against reference crops and run `construct_check.py` (`G1–G12`) before advancing.

## 5. SyncCraft Experimental Environment Evaluation & Pipeline Reform Blueprint

Direct inspection of SyncCraft's experimental audit engine (`src/synccraft/audit/sync_audit.py`) explains why models optimized strictly for `audit_scene(audit_profile="sculpted")` drift toward **simplified, surface-pasted, toy-like geometry** — and how to fix both our immediate workflow and SyncCraft's next revision.

### 5.1 Where SyncCraft `sync_audit.py` Is **Too Harsh / Over-Restrictive** (Blocking Master Carving)
1. **Parallel Stepped-Chamfer Cap (`MAX_SCULPTED_PARALLEL_OVERLAP_RATIO = 0.25` when `theta_deg < 5.0°`, line 437):**
   - *Artistic Impact:* ArtsByKev's *"Stepped Cuboid Chamfering"* (`WC33YHWU9NU`), Tetranadon's stepped sumo-belly slabs (`anchor_08`), and Amethyst Dragon's stepped snout scutes (`anchor_06`) rely on **parallel (`0°`) cuboids sunk `50–75%` into a host core** so only a `1–2u` step protrudes. Capping parallel overlap at `25%` of `t_med` forces `75%+` of the slab outside the body like a bulky Lego brick!
2. **Anti-Burial Rejection (`MAX_SCULPTED_BURIED_VOLUME_RATIO = 0.65` -> `swallowed_redundant_box`, line 484):**
   - *Artistic Impact:* Directly bans Darto 3d's `75%`-sunken Rathalos thigh scutes (`Z58DMwfZtx4` `01:08`) and Deviljho's `65–80%`-embedded chin osteoderms (`anchor_07`, `IzCwIiYj-Xk` `03:26`), forcing spikes and muscles to stick cartoonishly far out from the surface.
3. **Surface-Only `place()` / `attach()` Contact Law (`max_inset_depth = 0.5u` + `center_buried` rejection, lines 549–560):**
   - *Artistic Impact:* Forbids placing a secondary muscle or armor box's center inside its parent OBB via `place()`, biasing Block-DSL authors toward **outer-wall "sticker" attachment (`anchor = ±1`)**.
4. **Zero Sibling-Bone Immunity (`_sculpted_exemption_reason`, lines 374–391):**
   - *Artistic Impact:* Two neighboring carapace-row bones or muscle-group bones parented to `chest` (`deltoid_l` vs `trapezius_l`) trigger fatal SAT `illegal_intersection` unless every pairwise combination ($O(N^2)$) is manually listed in `blend_with`.

### 5.2 Where SyncCraft `sync_audit.py` Is **Too Loose** (Letting Toy Models Pass `100%`)
- `audit_scene` only enforces *negative physical non-intersection*, grounding (`y_min = 0`), and bone budget ceilings. A stick-figure dragon with `1` box per leg, `3` parallel peg toes, tall stilt posture, `0.40x`-chord gapped strip wings, a cloned box-snout, or even `0.3u` fake-Blender wafer slices passes `audit_scene` with **`0` errors**!
- That gap is now closed engine-agnostically by **`scripts/construct_check.py` v20 (`G1–G12`)**, which runs directly on **`.bbmodel`, Bedrock `.geo.json`, AND `.blockdsl`**.

### 5.3 Actionable SyncCraft vNext Upgrade Specification (And Immediate Workaround)
- **Immediate Workaround When Authoring in Current Read-Only SyncCraft:**
  - Never use `place(anchor=±1)` for compound musculature or sunken osteoderms; author explicit bone-local coordinates (`box ... from=[...] to=[...]`), group tightly interlocking bevel/muscle clusters using `prism_group: "<anatomical_cluster>"` (which `sync_audit.py` lines `375` & `438–455` **already exempts from `0.65` burial and `0.25` parallel overlap caps!**) + cross-bone `blend_with`, while keeping **inter-limb / wing-to-body** swept 15-axis OBB SAT 100% active!
- **Recommended Permanent SyncCraft Engine Reform (for the SyncCraft repo roadmap):**
  1. Promote `prism_group` into a first-class **`sculpt_group` / `anatomical_cluster`** attribute on bones and boxes: allow `buried_volume_fraction <= 0.88` (requiring only `min_exterior_protrusion >= 0.5u` so truly `100%`-invisible internal boxes are still caught!) and allow `0°` parallel stepped-chamfer embedding within the same `sculpt_group`.
