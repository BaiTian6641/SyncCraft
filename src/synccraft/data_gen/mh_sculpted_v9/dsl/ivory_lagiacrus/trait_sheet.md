| **1. Whole Gesture** | Terrestrial-adapted coastal Leviathan (`~7.4 HL` arc). Compared to pelagic Lagiacrus, Ivory Lagiacrus holds its ribcage and pelvis in a **slightly higher, muscular semi-sprawled land-striding crouch** (`pelvis` at `Y ≈ 11.6u`, `chest` at `Y ≈ 12.2u`, ventral scutes `~4.0u` off `Y = 0`), poised forward in a counter-coiled ambush S-wave to sprint and body-check across dry land. | **Keep & Exaggerate (`1.2x` shoulder/limb muscularity + counter-coiled terrestrial lunge poise)**. Distinct silhouette from marine `lagiacrus` while preserving authentic low-slung Leviathan proportions. |
| **2. Head, U-Snout & Swept Horned Hood** | Pearl-ivory armored crocodilian skull banded with royal-azure & slate-amethyst markings; long wedged U-snout (`snout_taper_ratio < 0.36`) with heavy crushing conical fangs; **longer swept ivory-to-amethyst crown horns**; and a **swept horned cobra hood (`hood_l` / `hood_r`)** featuring thicker backward-swept cobalt-banded ribs and golden-cream gill membranes. | **Keep & Exaggerate (`1.3x` swept horn length & horned hood barbs)**. Sculpt 2-stage wedged maxilla/rostrum + raised crocodilian nostril bosses. |
| **3. Neck & Reinforced Torso** | Stout, heavily muscled 3-segment S-neck (`neck_1..3`) and **broader reinforced shoulder & flank carapace shingles** alternating pearl-ivory and royal-azure/slate-amethyst bands (matching the blue-striped ivory carapace in `ivory_lagiacrus_reference.png`), over wide sunlit sand-cream ventral scutes. | **Exaggerate (`1.25x` shoulder pauldron & rib shingle mass)** to read immediately as a terrestrial brawler Leviathan. |
| **4. Twin-Pronged Dorsal Shock-Spires (Signature)** | **Enlarged, bifurcated (twin-pronged) slate-amethyst & cobalt dorsal shock-shell spires** (`shock_spire_1..4_l/r`) evolved to store and discharge massive bio-electricity on dry land without water conductivity, glowing with **sky-cyan & amber-violet cores**. | **Exaggerate (`1.35x` spire girth + V-forked twin prongs on thoracic/lumbar dynamos)** — unmistakable silhouette upgrade over standard Lagiacrus. |
| **5. Land-Striding Amphibious Limbs** | Thicker, semi-sprawled (`+Rz = 54°` fore, `52°` hind) muscular limbs with reinforced ivory/amethyst forearm bracer spurs and stout 4-toed / 3-toed clawed land-surf paddles planted flat on `Y = 0`. | **Keep & Exaggerate (`1.25x` bicep/bracer thickness & digging talons)**. |
| **6. Armored Paddle-Blade Tail** | Heavy 6-segment S-coiled crocodilian tail (`tail_1..6`) with thicker terrestrial lateral osteoderm keels and a sleek scalloped slate-amethyst & pearl-ivory caudal fin blade. | **Keep**; taper cleanly into an amethyst-tipped lance blade. |

Vibrant Surface Palette** | Dominant **Pearl-Ivory** carapace (`OKLCH [0.86, 0.038, 88]`), bold **Royal Cobalt-Azure** stripes (`[0.48, 0.145, 252]`) & **Slate-Amethyst** shock-shells (`[0.37, 0.118, 284]`), **Sunlit Sand-Gold** ventral scutes & hood webs (`[0.82, 0.088, 84]`), plus **Sky-Lightning Cyan** (`[0.87, 0.162, 214]`, emissive) & **Amber-Gold** eyes (`[0.80, 0.165, 72]`, emissive). | **Paint** vivid 6-step hue-shifted cel ramps (`cool violet-indigo shadows on ivory -> crisp pearl/cobalt/amethyst midtones -> warm sunlit cream highlights`) with zero grey desaturation. |
